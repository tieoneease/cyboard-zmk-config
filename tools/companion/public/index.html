What happens after arming?

The app waits for one compatible drive, compares its current image with the selected half’s backup, then submits the exact verified UF2 once. Keep this page open and do not unplug during writing.

Arming expires after two minutes or if the browser stops responding. A transfer is not a hardware behavior test. An interrupted transfer is reported as unverified and never retried automatically.
